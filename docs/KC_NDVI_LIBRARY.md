# Kc–NDVI library

CropWater-RS uses direct `Kc` relationships with NDVI.

Built-in records include equation, crop compatibility, reference, DOI where available, source type, verification status and calibration-domain note.

Verification status:
- `primary-source verified`
- `primary-source audit pending`
- `user-defined / unverified`

Users are responsible for evaluating transferability to their crop, sensor, climate, management and phenological context.

Built-in models currently include Kamble et al. (2013), Gontia & Tiwari (2010), Rocha et al. (2012), Singh & Irmak (2009), Reyes-González et al. (2015) and Rossi et al. (2010).
